How I work

The member experience, end to end.

SchoolsFirst's teller role combines personal service, transaction accuracy, cash controls, consultative referrals, and BSA awareness. These are the habits I practiced across branch banking and compliance.

01 / ACCURACY

Get the transaction right

Confirm identity and authorization, check account details, process the transaction, and explain the result clearly.

02 / SERVICE

Answer the real question

Resolve account and payment questions face to face, then guide customers through the mobile banking app when useful.

03 / REFERRALS

Spot a relevant next step

Listen for the need behind the request and refer suitable account or credit card options without pressuring the customer.

04 / CONTROLS

Protect member assets

Apply cash, identity, privacy, and BSA procedures during high-volume branch work.

05 / ESCALATION

Act on unusual activity

Record concerning account behavior in bank systems and route it to the appropriate team for review.

06 / FOLLOW THROUGH

Close the loop

Research discrepancies, coordinate with colleagues, and make the next step understandable to the customer.
