Operating method

A practical coordination loop.

This is how I approach administrative and program support when the work involves many requests and sensitive records.

01 / INTAKE

Understand the request

Capture the question, deadline and missing context before assigning a next step.

02 / VERIFY

Check the source

Confirm names, documents, records and data against the available source.

03 / ROUTE

Connect the owner

Resolve routine issues or pass a complete handoff to the right colleague.

04 / CLOSE

Record the outcome

Update the tracking record so the next person knows what happened.
