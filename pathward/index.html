WRITING SAMPLE

Objective, chronological, decision-ready.

SAR-STYLE SAMPLE
During a 90-day review, five linked accounts demonstrated activity inconsistent with stated purposes and expected transaction profiles. A business account received substantial credits from unrelated parties and rapidly transferred funds to related personal accounts, another business and newly observed foreign beneficiaries. Additional activity included repeated cash deposits below the CTR threshold, rapid P2P dispersal, business-to-personal transfers without documented purpose and near-immediate outbound wires. Shared counterparties, transaction velocity, KYC inconsistencies and insufficient supporting documentation support escalation for further BSA/AML review and SAR consideration.

The narrative identifies subjects and scope, summarizes the flow of funds, states objective red flags, avoids unsupported criminal conclusions and explains why the activity remains unresolved.

03 / INVESTIGATION METHOD

A repeatable case workflow.

Designed for high-volume workstreams where consistency, service levels, quality and defensible documentation matter.

1Review trigger

Identify why the alert fired and set investigation scope.

2Validate customer

Compare CIP/KYC, purpose, history, risk rating and expected activity.

3Reconstruct activity

Follow timing, velocity, channels, values, counterparties and geography.

4Research risk

Use prior alerts, OFAC/screening, OSINT, adverse media and records.

5Test explanations

Look for mitigating evidence and identify unresolved questions.

6Document decision

Close with rationale or escalate with evidence and SAR/EDD considerations.
