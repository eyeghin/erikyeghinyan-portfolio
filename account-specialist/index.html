How I handled the work

From intake to a complete record.

The sequence behind my onboarding, account review and escalation work.

01 / VERIFY

Confirm identity.

Compare customer details, live video verification and submitted documentation to CIP/KYC requirements.

02 / COMPLETE

Track missing items.

Record incomplete fields, request corrections and follow up before closing an onboarding review.

03 / REVIEW

Check the account.

Compare customer profile, checks, ACH/P2P or digital payments with expected behavior and supporting records.

04 / ESCALATE

Write the handoff.

Document unusual activity, OFAC results or fraud indicators and send evidence to the BSA or partner team.
