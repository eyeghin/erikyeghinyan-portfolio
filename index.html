MY METHODOLOGY

From alert to answer.

An investigation should be clear enough for a reviewer to understand both the evidence and the reasoning behind the recommendation.

01

Triage

Assess trigger, customer risk, urgency and potentially linked activity.

02

Trace

Follow payment origin, destination, velocity, timing and unusual patterns.

03

Validate

Compare KYC/CDD records, customer history and permitted open-source research.

04

Decide

Document a supported clear, monitor or escalate recommendation, including UAR/SAR support where appropriate.

05

Improve

Capture root causes, quality observations and potential detection-rule refinements.
