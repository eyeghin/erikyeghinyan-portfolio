INVESTIGATION THINKING

How I approach a case.

These are illustrative, anonymized workflows—not actual customer records or claims of SAR filing authority.

01 / ACH & P2P alert

Reconstruct the transaction sequence and compare against the expected customer profile.

Open investigation workflow ↗
  1. Identify triggering pattern and alert root cause.
  2. Trace counterparties, timing, sources and uses of funds.
  3. Document supporting evidence and remaining risk indicators.
  4. Dismiss with rationale or escalate for further review.

02 / High-risk onboarding

Evaluate identity consistency, stated activity and public-record risk before an EDD referral.

Open review workflow ↗
  1. Verify identity and ownership information.
  2. Review PEP, OFAC and adverse-media matches.
  3. Seek clarifying documents, resolve discrepancies and update risk notes.

03 / SAR referral

Make the case clear enough for an independent reviewer to reconstruct.

Open evidence workflow ↗
  1. Organize transaction chronology and counterparties.
  2. Explain the activity, red flags and relevant customer context.
  3. Attach evidence and escalate to authorized SAR decision-makers.
