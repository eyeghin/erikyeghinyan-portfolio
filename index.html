What I actually reviewed

Four moves inside an investigation.

The goal is a decision another reviewer can reconstruct, even when the activity is ambiguous.

01 / ACTIVITY

Read the signal.

Look at ACH and P2P activity, volume, frequency, counterparties and possible account takeover indicators.

02 / CUSTOMER

Check the baseline.

Compare alert activity with transaction history, customer profile, KYC/CDD information and prior behavior.

03 / RESEARCH

Test alternatives.

Use screening and open sources to investigate risk indicators, possible matches and mitigating context.

04 / DECISION

Leave a trail.

Record the transaction timeline, evidence, risk rationale and unresolved questions in Unit21 for closure or referral.
