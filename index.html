ILLUSTRATIVE WORKFLOW

Start with
the account records.

Before contacting a merchant, I would establish what is owed, what has already been paid, what is disputed and which action I am authorized to take.

Example outstanding balance$1,250.00

$1,800 returned item − $600 applied payment + $50 documented fee

Fictional reconciliation example. TSYS, HappyFox and merchant reserves are role-specific workflows I would learn through employer training.

01Validate the return & account

Match the return record to the merchant, original payment, bank account details, reason and account status. Confirm the underlying records before outreach.

02Reconcile the balance

Compare the ledger with Accounts Receivable. Verify applied payments, refunds, chargebacks, adjustments and supported fees; investigate missing or misallocated payments.

03Follow the reserve procedure

Apply a 100% reserve through TSYS when required and authorized by the employer’s process. Document the action and approval; explain release conditions only within delegated authority.

04Contact & document

Use the approved phone and HappyFox email workflow. Explain the verified breakdown, listen to the merchant’s circumstances and record any dispute, arrangement, promise to pay and follow-up date.

05Coordinate & confirm resolution

Work with Merchant Maintenance, Partner Support, Merchant Success and Risk. Use permitted public contact research when appropriate, verify payments are correctly applied and confirm the cleared balance.
