How I investigate risk

Representative, sanitized work samples based on real AML and fraud workflows. No customer or confidential information is included.

01

Transaction monitoring

From alert to defensible decision

A practical workflow for reviewing unusual ACH and P2P activity without treating every anomaly as suspicious.

Signal

New-device access, rapid movement, beneficiary concentration and behavior inconsistent with the expected account profile.

Approach

Reconstructed the sequence, compared activity with the customer profile, reviewed counterparties and documented supporting and mitigating evidence.

Deliverable

A clear escalation recommendation and audit-ready rationale separating observable facts from analyst judgment.

02

Fraud operations

Account takeover pattern review

A structured review of authentication and payment behavior to identify takeover indicators while preserving legitimate activity.

Signal

Credential or device changes followed by unfamiliar transfers, unusual velocity and deviations from normal patterns.

Approach

Connected access events with transactional behavior, prioritized high-risk indicators and separated fraud evidence from normal variation.

Deliverable

A concise disposition with the key timeline, risk factors and next-best action for escalation or closure.

03

Customer due diligence

Risk-based onboarding review

A KYC and enhanced-due-diligence approach that turns scattered customer information into a coherent risk picture.

Signal

Identity inconsistencies, higher-risk geography, unclear purpose, adverse media or activity outside the stated profile.

Approach

Validated identity, reviewed business context, screened relevant parties and documented red flags and reasonable explanations.

Deliverable

A transparent risk assessment with traceable evidence and a proportionate recommendation.
