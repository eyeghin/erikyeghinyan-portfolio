Client conversations & referrals

A useful referral starts
with a better question.

My experience includes credit card and CD discussions, 5–10 daily outreach calls, and high-balance client referrals. These illustrative conversations show how I approach the work.

01 / Savings & CDs

Understand the timeline.

“When do you expect to need these funds, and how important is having immediate access?”

I would clarify the goal, liquidity needs, and timeframe before discussing an appropriate savings or CD conversation.

The referral & follow-up

With the client’s agreement, introduce a senior banker with the goal, timeframe, access needs, and questions. Confirm current product terms and arrange the next step without promising a rate or outcome.

02 / Credit card needs

Find the actual priority.

“What would you like your card to do better for you—manage everyday spending, simplify payments, or support another goal?”

I would listen first, explain relevant features and costs within my role, and check understanding before moving toward an application or referral.

The referral & follow-up

Pass the client’s priorities and unresolved questions to the appropriate banker. Make the next step clear, without suggesting guaranteed approval or a credit limit.

03 / High-balance clients

Connect the right expertise.

“Are these funds set aside for a specific goal, or would a conversation about your options be useful?”

I would use the balance as a reason to ask a thoughtful question, then understand the client’s priorities and preferred next step.

The referral & follow-up

Make a warm introduction to a senior banker or authorized specialist, share the client’s goal and context, and confirm how the team will follow up. Specialized advice stays with the appropriately licensed colleague.

04 · Close the loop
