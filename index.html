From alert to rationale

A decision is only
as useful as
its explanation.

My approach is to identify what is unusual, test plausible explanations and leave an evidence trail that makes the outcome understandable.

Case-writing sample · Synthetic

“The account received transfers from multiple previously unseen senders, followed by rapid outgoing payments. The available profile does not explain the change in activity. Further review should establish the relationships between parties and the purpose of the transfers before determining disposition.”

Observation → context gap → specific next step

Establish the baseline.

Confirm the trigger, review period, customer profile and expected activity. Check previous reviews and relevant account history.

Reconstruct the movement.

Trace incoming and outgoing funds, timing, payment channels, counterparties and balances. Look for linked activity and repeated patterns.

Test the explanation.

Compare evidence with the stated purpose of the account. Research relevant parties and screening results, assess mitigating factors and record what is still unknown.

Document and escalate appropriately.

Record the facts and rationale, attach supporting evidence and route the review within policy and SLA. Support SAR preparation where required; authorized teams determine filing.
