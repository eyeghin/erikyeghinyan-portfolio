How I actually worked

The steps behind a documented decision.

These are tasks drawn from my experience. I cannot share employer records or individual customer cases.

01 / Monitor

Review account activity and ACH/P2P transactions; compare amounts, frequency, counterparties and possible account takeover indicators with earlier behavior.

02 / Establish context

Check customer history, KYC/CDD information and risk profile against the flagged activity before calling it suspicious.

03 / Research

Review World-Check findings and open sources, connect relevant information to the transaction timeline and identify unresolved questions.

04 / Document

Write a Unit21 narrative with the activity, evidence and risk rationale supporting closure or a suspicious activity referral.

05 / Route

Preserve the reasons and outstanding questions for the next fraud or compliance reviewer when escalation is warranted.
