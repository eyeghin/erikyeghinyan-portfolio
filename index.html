01 / Support in action

What I say when a customer is confused.

Illustrative support conversations show my approach. They are examples, not transcripts or claims about specific past customers.

PAYMENTS · CUSTOMER WORRIED

“Where did my transfer go?”

I would verify the customer, check transaction status and timing, distinguish a pending transfer from a completed one, then explain the next step without promising an outcome I cannot confirm.

1 Confirm identity and transfer details
2 Review status and available payment history
3 Explain timing or route a trace/escalation
Client care Example dialogue · not an actual chat
“It says pending, but the money is gone. What's happening?”
“I understand why that's concerning. After I verify your account, I'll check the transfer status and the date it was sent. Then I can tell you what is confirmed, what is still pending, and when to follow up.”
If the status does not match the expected timeline, I would document the details and route the case to the right team.
