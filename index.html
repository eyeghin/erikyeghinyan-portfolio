Digital service / practical approach

Help that fits the client.

01 / Mobile-app access

“Let’s check the message you see. I’ll guide you through the bank’s supported recovery steps at your pace, then confirm you can access what you need.”

Use approved verification; keep passwords and one-time codes private.
02 / New-account documentation

“Let’s confirm the account’s purpose, who is authorized and which documents are required. I’ll explain any missing items and the next step.”

I follow the bank’s documentation and authorization requirements.
03 / Needs-based referrals

“What are you hoping to improve: everyday banking, savings or managing business payments? I can connect you with the appropriate colleague and share your goal.”

Deposit, lending and treasury solutions require a relevant needs assessment and specialist handoff.
04 / Operational accuracy

Check transaction details and authorization, balance the teller drawer, preserve supporting records and route discrepancies promptly.

I follow the bank’s procedures for records, operational checks and exception handling.
05 / Sensitive risk communications

“I understand you need a clear answer. Let’s verify the account details and identify the next step available under the bank’s process. I’ll document your concern and route any review that requires the risk team.”

Illustrative response for an account review, restriction or closure inquiry. Protect confidential review information and avoid unsupported promises.
06 / A handoff the next team can use

Record the customer’s question, verification completed, relevant account or transaction facts, action taken and outstanding items. Send the appropriate team enough context to continue without repeating the investigation.

Investigation workflow

Listen. Verify. Resolve. Follow through.

My approach connects customer experience with AML, fraud, sanctions, onboarding and ongoing due diligence. Clear explanations and complete handoffs matter at every step.

01 / TRIAGE

Understand the need.

Ask what the client needs: a transaction, account question, digital access or a product conversation.

02 / CONTEXT

Verify the details.

Confirm identity, authorization and supporting documents; resolve missing or inconsistent information.

03 / EVIDENCE

Resolve or refer.

Explain the available solution and connect the appropriate banker, operations or BSA specialist with context.

04 / ACTION

Complete the follow-up.

Record the action and outstanding items, confirm the next step and review exceptions under procedure.
