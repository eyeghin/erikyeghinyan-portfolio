How I work

The review behind the result.

A good disposition shows what changed, what the evidence supports, and what still needs investigation.

01 / Establish a baseline

Compare activity with the customer.

Review expected use, transaction history, counterparties and KYC details before deciding what an alert means.

02 / Test the pattern

Follow the money and the timing.

Check channels such as ACH and P2P, activity changes, research findings and plausible explanations.

03 / Make it reviewable

Show the evidence behind the call.

Document the facts, unresolved questions and escalation rationale in a case narrative that another reviewer can follow.
