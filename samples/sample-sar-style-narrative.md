# Sample SAR-Style Narrative

> Educational writing sample only. This is not an actual SAR and is not based on real activity. Filing decisions and narratives must follow an institution's approved procedures and applicable law.

## Fictional Narrative

Example Bank reviewed consumer checking account ending 4821 held by Jordan Lee after transaction-monitoring systems identified rapid movement of ACH credits to multiple P2P recipients. From March 1 through March 30, 2026, the account received eight ACH credits totaling $18,740 from five business originators with no apparent relationship to Lee. Within 24 hours of receipt, Lee transferred $17,050 through 23 P2P payments to eleven individuals, including six recipients not previously observed on the account. Individual payments ranged from $300 to $1,500 and frequently appeared in round-dollar amounts.

The reviewed activity differed from the account's prior pattern. During the preceding six months, the account primarily received one biweekly payroll deposit from a single employer and was used for debit-card purchases, recurring household payments, and occasional P2P transfers to two recipients. Available KYC information identified Lee as a retail employee with expected monthly account activity of approximately $3,500.

Open-source research did not identify a clear relationship between Lee and the ACH originators. Example Bank found no confirmed sanctions matches involving Lee or the reviewed counterparties. The source and purpose of the incoming business payments and the rapid transfers to multiple individuals could not be reasonably determined from available account records.

Example Bank escalated the activity because the substantial change from expected behavior, multiple unrelated originators, rapid movement of funds, and newly introduced P2P recipients indicated potential pass-through activity. Supporting transaction records are available upon appropriate request.

## Why This Structure Works

- Identifies the subject, account, review period, and alert reason
- States transaction totals, channels, and counterparty counts
- Compares the activity with the historical customer profile
- Separates verified facts from unresolved questions
- Explains the escalation rationale without claiming an unproven offense
- Uses chronological, concise, and objective language
