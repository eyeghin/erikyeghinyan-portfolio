Investigation writing sample

Rapid ACH credits followed by P2P transfers

Fictional training scenario. This example contains no real customer or employer data. It illustrates an analytical approach, not an actual investigation or SAR filing decision.

$18,740Eight ACH credits
91%Moved within 24 hours
11P2P recipients

Alert and baseline

A consumer checking account received eight ACH credits from five unrelated business originators over 30 days. About 91% of the incoming funds moved to eleven P2P recipients within 24 hours. The customer had described the account as a payroll and household spending account. Prior activity consisted mainly of one biweekly payroll deposit, debit-card purchases and occasional transfers to two recurring recipients.

Review plan

  1. Confirm the alert logic and reconstruct the full transaction timeline.
  2. Compare volume, velocity, counterparties and payment channels with the customer’s historical activity and KYC profile.
  3. Research originators and relevant counterparties using approved open sources.
  4. Check prior alerts, returns, disputes, customer contact and account-access anomalies.
  5. Record both risk indicators and information that could explain the change.

Assessment

The unrelated originators, rapid movement of funds, new recipients and low retained balance warrant further review. No confirmed sanctions match or conclusive public-source evidence is present in the fictional record. The pattern alone does not establish account takeover or an underlying offense.

Disposition

Escalate for enhanced review. Document the transaction pattern, the departure from expected use, the checks completed and the unresolved purpose of the business payments. Additional review or customer outreach should follow applicable institutional procedures.
