05 / Cape Cod 5 service desk

How I would handle it.

Each scenario reflects the Client Care Specialist I priorities: superior service, digital adoption, client authentication, fraud awareness, accurate CRM notes, defined authority, and useful next steps.

Digital support

“I can’t get into online banking.”

  1. Listen and diagnose.
    Clarify the device, channel, message, and steps already attempted.
  2. Authenticate securely.
    Follow Bank guidelines before discussing account-specific information.
  3. Guide one step at a time.
    Support setup, navigation, or maintenance without overwhelming the client.
  4. Document and confirm.
    Record the interaction accurately and verify that the client can move forward.
Fraud awareness

“I don’t recognize this transaction.”

  1. Protect first.
    Authenticate the client and follow the approved security process.
  2. Gather precise facts.
    Clarify the concern without leading the client or promising an outcome.
  3. Stay within authority.
    Take the permitted action and request higher-level approval or specialist help when needed.
  4. Set expectations.
    Explain the next step, document the concern, and provide a clear handoff.
Needs-based service

“Is there an easier way to do this?”

  1. Understand the goal.
    Ask what the client is trying to accomplish before suggesting a product.
  2. Match the actual need.
    Explain the relevant banking service or digital option using approved information.
  3. Be clear and accurate.
    Explain requirements and involve the correct colleague when needed.
  4. Respect the choice.
    Answer questions without pressure and record the agreed next step.
