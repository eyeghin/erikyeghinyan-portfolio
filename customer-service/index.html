Listen → Clarify → Resolve → Follow up

“I’ve already called about this.”

  1. Recognize the frustration.
    Let the customer explain. Acknowledge the inconvenience without guessing what went wrong.
  2. Establish the facts securely.
    Complete required verification, review available notes, and confirm the issue in plain language.
  3. Act within my authority.
    Resolve what I can under policy. If specialist review is needed, explain the next step and provide a clear handoff.
  4. Close the loop.
    Document the interaction, set accurate expectations, and follow the bank’s follow-up process.

Experience I draw on: restaurant service recovery, multilingual support, and frontline banking.

Understand → Route → Document → Follow through

“Who can help with my loan?”

  1. Clarify the request.
    Understand whether the customer needs an originator, loan servicing, deposit services, or another team.
  2. Protect the information.
    Follow verification and privacy procedures before discussing account-specific details.
  3. Make the handoff useful.
    Use the approved transfer process and share relevant context securely so the customer does not start over unnecessarily.
  4. Avoid a dead end.
    If the specialist is unavailable, explain the approved next step and document the request for follow-up.

Experience I draw on: coordinating guests, kitchen teams, customer-support conversations, and banking referrals.

Ask → Understand → Explain → Respect the choice

“Is there an easier way to do this?”

  1. Start with the need.
    Ask what the customer is trying to accomplish before mentioning another product.
  2. Offer a relevant option.
    Use current, approved product information to explain a service that may help.
  3. Be clear about the details.
    Explain applicable requirements and fees accurately, or refer to a qualified colleague when needed.
  4. Let the customer decide.
    Answer questions without pressure and document any agreed next step.

Experience I draw on: hospitality recommendations, Hollister retail service, IDBank operations, and Btalk outreach.
