01 / WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Not a script.
A better answer.

The goal isn't to close a conversation quickly. It's to make the experience feel clear, respectful and complete—without losing procedural accuracy, privacy or ownership.

01 / CONNECTION
♡

Ask the right questions, hear the real concern, and make the customer feel understood.

02 / PRECISION
◎

Verify account context, navigate controls and keep every explanation accurate.

03 / OWNERSHIP
↗

Resolve what is in scope. Escalate responsibly. Keep the handoff useful and actionable.
