      01 / Story direction

      Built to feel like an actual investigation.

      The goal was simple: make the page feel impressive without becoming decorative. This design uses darkness, tension, and restrained motion to communicate what financial-crime work actually feels like—reading unclear patterns, resisting easy conclusions, and building a rationale that can stand up to scrutiny.

      Signal
      Not every alert deserves drama.

      The important work is finding which details matter, and which details are just noise. The design mirrors that shift from ambiguity to clarity.

      Network
      Relationships tell the real story.

      Customers, counterparties, devices, branches, timing, and amounts become more powerful once they are viewed together instead of in isolation.

      Decision
      Escalation requires discipline.

      This portfolio uses fictional cases to show how I think through review, documentation, and escalation—not to overclaim experience or use real data.
