01 / The point of view

Follow the evidence. Question the pattern.

Strong investigations begin with disciplined observation. I review the expected activity, connect the relevant accounts and counterparties, assess reasonable explanations, and write clear findings rather than relying on a risk score alone.

Transaction monitoring, KYC/CDD/EDD review support, branch banking operations, alert analysis, documentation, and escalations tied to potential suspicious activity. I focus on patterns, rationale, and consistency.
