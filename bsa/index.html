01 / Tools, transaction review & narratives

How I build a case.

From the alert to a traceable decision: what I checked, what the data showed, and what I documented.

01 · Understand the alert

Identify the rule, review window, payment channel and triggering activity. Check transaction status before totaling successful credits and debits.

02 · Establish context

Review KYC, expected account purpose, historical activity, linked payment instruments and available customer records. Document missing information.

03 · Reconstruct the flow

Separate inflows from outflows. Aggregate counterparties, amounts and counts in Excel. Examine timing, concentration and repeated movement.

04 · Test explanations

Compare red flags with mitigating facts. Avoid assuming that unfamiliar senders, round amounts or multiple states alone prove suspicious activity.

05 · Document & route

Record totals, chronology, research, remaining concerns and the reason for disposition. Refer unresolved risk for second-line review and address QA feedback.
