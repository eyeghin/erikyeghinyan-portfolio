My review method

Evidence first. Assumptions last.

A consistent five-step process keeps high-volume investigations clear, accurate and ready for QA or escalation.

01

Understand the alert

Identify the rule, review period, channel and triggering activity.

02

Establish context

Review KYC, intended use, history, risk profile and linked information.

03

Rebuild the flow

Separate credits and debits; quantify amounts, timing and counterparties.

04

Test explanations

Compare red flags with mitigating facts and identify what remains unresolved.

05

Document & route

Write the rationale, cite evidence and recommend closure or further review.
