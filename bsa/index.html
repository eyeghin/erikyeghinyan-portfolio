How I investigate

Make the next decision easier.

A practical workflow from alert intake through reviewable evidence and the right escalation.

01 / TRIAGE

Read the trigger.

Review reports, logs and transaction data. Check amount, timing, frequency, channel and prior behavior.

02 / CONTEXT

Know the account.

Compare CIP, KYC/CDD, expected activity, counterparties, entities and relevant screening findings.

03 / EVIDENCE

Write the trail.

Compile timelines, interview notes, mitigating context, unresolved questions and the reason for disposition.

04 / ACTION

Close or escalate.

Route suspicious activity, assess CTR or SAR reporting, resolve OFAC alerts and preserve audit-ready records.
