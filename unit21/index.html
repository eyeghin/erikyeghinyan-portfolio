03 / Written judgement

A defensible narrative
is an evidence trail.

Flex is looking for analysts who write so clearly that supervisors and bank partners can follow the decision without a follow-up call. My case documentation starts with the trigger and ends with an action.

01 / ESTABLISH

Why the alert exists

Identify the monitoring scenario, time window, transaction behavior and customer baseline. Use observed facts rather than suspicion alone.

02 / SUBSTANTIATE

What the records actually show

Compare parties, movement, source of funds, access indicators, onboarding information, and research. Note both corroboration and unresolved gaps.

03 / DISPOSITION

Why the decision follows

Close when a legitimate explanation is supported. Escalate when material concerns persist. Document why, what to review next and who owns the follow-up.
